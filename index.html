Methodology

Data compiled 7 September 2026 from Wikipedia driver and championship pages, fiaformula2.com, fiaformula3.com, official FRECA/Italian F4 sources, and race reports from Formula1.com, Pit Debrief and Formula Scout. "Flagship season" is each driver's main single-seater program that year; parallel/secondary programs aren't shown. Karting depth differs by tier: FIA F2 drivers show only their final karting season (it's the furthest back and least relevant to their current form); FIA F3, FRECA and Italian F4 drivers show their final two. This is a compiled snapshot, not a live feed — ask for a refresh after results change.

Comparison is capped at three drivers at once, both for chart legibility and so each gets a colour that stays distinguishable, including for colour-blind readers, in every pairing.
